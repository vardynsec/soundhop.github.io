Sayline Privacy Policy

Cobaltline Software · Effective October 29, 2026

Sayline is a teleprompter for Windows. It shows your script in a small window under your webcam, can follow your voice as you read, and can be controlled from your phone on the same Wi-Fi. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: Sayline does not collect, store or transmit any personal information to us or to anyone else. Your voice is processed on your PC and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Your voice and the microphone

  • When "Follow my voice" is on, Sayline listens to the microphone while the prompter is running, so it can move the text along as you read. It stops listening when you pause, stop or close the prompter.
  • Speech is recognized on your PC by the speech recognizer built into Windows for the script's language. Sayline does not use an online speech service, and no audio or text of what you said leaves your PC.
  • Sayline does not record or save audio, and does not keep a transcript. The words heard are compared with your script in memory and then discarded.
  • Windows may keep its own local speech recognition profile on your PC, as it does for any app that uses Windows speech recognition. Sayline does not read or send it.
  • You can turn the microphone off for Sayline at any time in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone. The prompter then scrolls at a steady pace.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No cloud. Scripts stay on your PC.

Data that stays on your PC

  • Scripts. The scripts you write, paste or import are saved in the app's local data folder, with their title, language, last change and where you stopped reading. Deleting a script removes it; uninstalling the app removes all of them.
  • Imported files. When you open a Word document or a PowerPoint presentation, Sayline reads its text (for PowerPoint, the speaker notes) on your PC and saves a copy of that text as a script. The original file is not changed or uploaded.
  • Check now. To show you what a screen share sees, Sayline takes a capture of the area around the prompter using the same Windows capture methods meeting apps use. The capture is shown to you and then discarded; it is not saved or sent.
  • Settings (text size, speed, position, theme, language, shortcuts and your other choices) are saved in the app's local data folder.
  • Logs. If something goes wrong, a short technical log is written to the local data folder. It contains no script text and no speech. It is never sent automatically; you can open the folder from Settings if you choose to share it with support.

Phone remote

  • When you open "Use your phone as a remote", Sayline serves a small control page from your PC on your local network. The phone opens it by scanning a QR code; nothing is installed on the phone.
  • Only devices with local network addresses can reach the page, and only with the one-time code in the current QR code, which changes after each phone connects and every 10 minutes. A connected phone can start, pause and steer the prompter and see the current line of your script; it cannot see your files or anything else on your PC.
  • The phone's browser holds a session cookie that only works with your PC and ends when you disconnect, quit Sayline or after two hours. The phone's name shown in the app ("iPhone", "Android") comes from its browser and is kept only while it is connected.

Network permissions

The app installs a Windows Firewall rule that lets devices on your local network reach the phone remote page (TCP ports 52740 to 52749). The rule only applies to Sayline and is removed when you uninstall the app. Sayline makes no other network connections, except the Microsoft Store's own check of your purchase.

Purchases

Sayline is a free download. Following your voice, the phone remote and PowerPoint import are a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app only asks the Microsoft Store whether the purchase exists on your account; it never sees your payment details. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to the purchase itself.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
