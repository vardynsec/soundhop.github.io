Showsteps Privacy Policy

Cobaltline Software · Effective October 21, 2026

Showsteps records the steps you take in apps on your Windows PC and turns them into a how-to guide with a picture and a sentence for each step. Because a recording can show what is on your screen, this policy explains exactly what the app records, where it is kept and who can see it. The short version: Showsteps records only while you tell it to, never records what you type, keeps everything on your PC, and sends nothing anywhere unless you export or share a guide yourself.

When the app records

  • Only after you press Start recording (or the Ctrl+Alt+R shortcut). Nothing is recorded at any other time, and the app never starts a recording by itself.
  • While it records, a small bar with Pause and Stop stays at the top of the screen. It can't be hidden, and it never appears in the pictures.
  • Recording pauses by itself while a password manager or Windows Security is in front, and while the Windows sign-in or security screen is showing. You can add your own apps to this list in Settings.

What a recording contains

  • A picture for each step. When you click, the app takes a picture of the window you clicked (or of that screen, if you choose so in Settings). The picture shows whatever that window shows at that moment, which can include personal information, so please look over a guide before you share it.
  • The name of what you clicked, such as "Save" or "File name", read through the Windows accessibility interface, so the step can say "Click Save".
  • The app's name, the window title and the time of each step.
  • Notes you type into Showsteps yourself.

What the app never records

  • What you type. Showsteps does not listen to the keyboard at all. It notices that the contents of a box changed, so it can write "Type in the Name box", but it never reads the text. Boxes you typed in and password boxes are blurred in the pictures automatically; you can show a blurred box again in a single step if you want to.
  • Sound, video, the camera or the microphone.
  • Your other screens or windows that you didn't click.
  • Windows that run as administrator: Windows doesn't let normal apps see clicks there. The guide shows a gap instead, and you can capture a picture yourself with the Capture a step shortcut.

Where guides are kept

  • Guides and their pictures are saved only in the app's own data folder on your PC. Nothing is uploaded, synced or backed up by Showsteps.
  • You can delete any step, any guide, or all guides at once (Settings › Delete all). Uninstalling the app deletes its data folder with all guides; files you exported stay where you saved them.
  • Exports (Word, PDF, web page, Markdown, help-desk report) are written to the folder you choose (Documents\Showsteps unless you change it). Blur, crops and the marks you draw are merged into the exported pictures, so blurred content can't be recovered from an exported file.
  • Send to support saves a help-desk report and opens the Windows Share window. You choose the app and the person it goes to; Showsteps itself sends nothing.
  • Search the pictures (in the export window) looks for email addresses, phone numbers and card numbers with the text recognition built into Windows. It runs on your PC; the pictures are not sent anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No network connections of its own. The only connections are the Microsoft Store's license and purchase services, and the web pages you open from links in the app (such as this policy).

Settings and logs

Your settings (what each picture shows, private apps, export folder, theme and language) are saved in the app's local data folder. If something goes wrong, a short technical log is written there too. It holds technical details only (no pictures, and never anything you typed) and is never sent automatically; you can open the folder from Settings if you choose to share it with support.

Purchases

Showsteps is a free download. Exports of more than 15 steps per guide are a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app only asks the Microsoft Store whether the purchase exists on your account; it never sees your payment details. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to the purchase itself.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
