DriveTempo Privacy Policy

Cobaltline Software · Effective October 17, 2026

DriveTempo is a storage speed test for Windows. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: DriveTempo does not collect, store or transmit any personal information.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No network connections of its own. Benchmark results are never uploaded.

Data that stays on your PC

  • Settings (theme, language, last chosen drive, test size and duration) are saved in the app's local data folder.
  • Diagnostic logs (benchmark commands, errors and crash details) are saved in the app's local data folder for 7 days. They contain no personal information and are only shared if you choose to send them to us yourself.
  • Temporary test file. During a test the app writes a temporary file filled with random data to the drive you select and deletes it when the test ends.
  • Exports. Results are copied, saved or shared only when you choose to, to the place you choose.

Uninstalling the app removes its local data folder.

Microsoft Store

Purchases, the free trial and app ratings are handled by the Microsoft Store. The app asks the Store only whether it is licensed. Microsoft's handling of that data is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

隐私政策（简体中文）

DriveTempo 不收集、不存储、也不传输任何个人信息。应用没有帐户、没有统计或广告，也不会自行联网。设置和诊断日志只保存在本机的应用数据文件夹中（日志保留 7 天）。测试时写入的临时文件在测试结束后会被删除。购买、试用和评分由 Microsoft Store 处理。

Datenschutzerklärung (Deutsch)
