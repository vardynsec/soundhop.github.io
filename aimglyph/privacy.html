AimGlyph Privacy Policy

Cobaltline Software · Effective October 17, 2026

AimGlyph is a crosshair overlay for PC games on Windows. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: AimGlyph does not collect, store or transmit any personal information.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No network connections of its own. Nothing you do in the app is uploaded.
  • No access to game processes: nothing is injected into, read from or written to any game.
  • No keyboard logging: global shortcuts use the Windows hotkey API, which reports only the key combinations you chose.

Data that stays on your PC

  • Settings and crosshairs (your designs, saved crosshairs, favourites, shortcuts, theme and language) are saved in the app's local data folder.
  • Game names. To use the right crosshair for the game in front, the app reads the program file name and window title of the app in the foreground. Games you play are remembered in the local data folder (games without their own crosshair for 14 days) so you can give them a crosshair later. You can remove any game in the app.
  • Images you choose as a crosshair are copied, resized, into the local data folder.
  • "My game" preview. If you choose it, the app copies a small area around the centre of your screen to use as the preview background. It is kept in memory only and is gone when the app closes.
  • Diagnostic logs (errors and crash details) are saved in the local data folder for 7 days. They contain no personal information and are only shared if you choose to send them to us yourself.
  • Exports. Crosshairs are copied as share codes or saved as files only when you choose to, to the place you choose.

Uninstalling the app removes its local data folder.

Microsoft Store

Purchases, the trial and app ratings are handled by the Microsoft Store. The app asks the Store only whether it is licensed. Microsoft's handling of that data is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

隐私政策（简体中文）

AimGlyph 不收集、不存储、也不传输任何个人信息。应用没有帐户、没有统计或广告，也不会自行联网，更不会注入或读取任何游戏进程。为给前台游戏使用对应的准星，应用会读取前台程序的文件名和窗口标题；玩过的游戏、你的准星、设置和诊断日志（保留 7 天）只保存在本机的应用数据文件夹中。可选的“我的游戏”预览截图只保存在内存中，关闭应用即消失。购买、试用和评分由 Microsoft Store 处理。

Datenschutzerklärung (Deutsch)

AimGlyph erhebt, speichert und überträgt keine personenbezogenen Daten. Es gibt kein Konto, keine Analyse, keine Werbung; die App baut selbst keine Netzwerkverbindungen auf und greift nicht auf Spielprozesse zu. Um das passende Fadenkreuz für das Spiel im Vordergrund zu verwenden, liest sie den Programmnamen und Fenstertitel der Vordergrund-App. Gespielte Spiele, Ihre Fadenkreuze, Einstellungen und Diagnoseprotokolle (7 Tage) werden nur im lokalen App-Datenordner gespeichert. Die optionale „Mein Spiel“-Vorschauaufnahme bleibt nur im Arbeitsspeicher und verschwindet beim Schließen der App. Kauf, Testversion und Bewertungen wickelt der Microsoft Store ab.
