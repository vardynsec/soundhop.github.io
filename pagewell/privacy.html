Pagewell Privacy Policy

Cobaltline Software · Effective October 29, 2026

Pagewell opens Microsoft Publisher (.pub) files on your Windows PC, shows their pages, prints them and saves them as PDF, PNG or Word files. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: Pagewell does not collect, store or transmit any personal information to us or to anyone else, and your files never leave your PC.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No uploads. Files are read and converted on your PC; they never pass through our servers or any online service.
  • Pagewell only reads the files and folders you open, and only writes the files you ask it to save, where you choose.

Data that stays on your PC

  • Recent files and folders. The paths of the last files and folders you opened are kept so they appear under Recent in the sidebar, with a small picture of the first page when the file contains one. Right-click a recent item to remove it or to clear the whole list (which also deletes the cached pictures).
  • Pages while a file is open. To show a file, Pagewell writes its pages and pictures to a temporary folder on your PC. The folder is deleted when you close the app, and any leftovers are deleted the next time it starts. The page viewer keeps its own cache in the app's local data folder; uninstalling the app removes it, with everything else listed here.
  • Settings (theme, language, window size and position) are saved in the app's local data folder.
  • Logs. If something goes wrong, a short technical log is written to the local data folder. It may name the file involved, never its contents, and it is never sent automatically; you can open the folder from Settings if you choose to share it with support.

Components from Windows

Pagewell shows and prints pages with Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is part of Windows. WebView2 may download language data from Microsoft by itself, such as the dictionaries it uses to hyphenate text; no content from your files is sent. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to WebView2.

Purchases

Pagewell is a free download. Removing the "Saved with Pagewell" line from PDFs, and saving as PNG and Word, taking out pictures and converting whole folders, is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app only asks the Microsoft Store whether the purchase exists on your account; it never sees your payment details. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to the purchase itself.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes
