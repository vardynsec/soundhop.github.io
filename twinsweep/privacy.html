TwinSweep Privacy Policy

Cobaltline Software · Effective October 18, 2026

TwinSweep finds duplicate files on your Windows PC and moves the extra copies to the Recycle Bin. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: TwinSweep does not collect, store or transmit any personal information, and your files never leave your PC.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No uploads. The app makes no network connections of its own; file names, photos and scan results are never sent anywhere.
  • No permanent deletion. Copies go to the Windows Recycle Bin, or to a "TwinSweep Safety" folder on drives that have no Recycle Bin, so they can always be restored until you empty them yourself.

Data that stays on your PC

  • The folders you scan. The app reads the folders and drives you choose to find files with identical contents. Windows, installed programs, app data and cloud files that are not on your PC are never scanned.
  • Fingerprints. To make the next scan fast, the app remembers, for each file it checked, its path, size, date and a content fingerprint. They are saved in the app's local data folder. Settings › Forget remembered files deletes them.
  • Cleanup history. For Undo and History, the app records which copies it moved, from where and to where. You can remove these records by uninstalling the app.
  • Settings (folders you ticked, keep rules, excluded folders, theme and language) and the free version's allowance are saved in the local data folder.
  • Logs. If something goes wrong, a short technical log is written to the local data folder. It is never sent automatically; you can open the folder from Settings if you choose to share it with support.

Purchases

TwinSweep is a free download. Unlimited cleaning is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app only asks the Microsoft Store whether the purchase exists on your account; it never sees your payment details. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to the purchase itself.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
