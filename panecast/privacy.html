Panecast Privacy Policy

Cobaltline Software · Effective October 19, 2026

Panecast is a screen mirroring receiver for Windows: an iPhone, iPad or Mac on your local network can show its screen and play its sound on your PC. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: Panecast works only on your local network and does not collect, upload or share any personal information with us or anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No internet connections of its own. Nothing you mirror, record or snap is uploaded.

What happens on your local network

  • Announcing the PC. So that your devices can find it, Panecast announces this PC on the local network under its receiver name. The name is "<your first name>'s PC" by default, taken from your Windows user name, and you can change it at any time in the app. Devices on the same network can see this name.
  • Mirroring and music. The picture and sound your device sends are decoded and played on this PC as they arrive. They are not stored unless you record or take a snapshot. Movie and TV apps that protect their content are not received.
  • Who may connect. By default the app asks before a new device connects for the first time, and you can require a 4-digit code.
  • Phone check. If you open the connection check, the app serves a small page on your local network. When a phone opens it, the app uses the phone's local network address and browser description only to show "Your phone reached this PC". Nothing about it is saved.
  • Receiver engine. The receiving is done by PanecastEngine, an open-source helper program that is installed with the app and runs on your PC. It makes no internet connections either.

Data that stays on your PC

  • Devices. The names and model types of devices that connected, whether you allowed or blocked them, when each was last seen and how many sessions it had are saved in the app's local data folder, so the app can recognise them next time. You can forget any device in the app.
  • Receiver identity. A random device identifier and a key pair are created on first start and saved in the local data folder. Devices use them to recognise the receiver; they contain nothing about you.
  • Recordings and snapshots are saved only when you click Record or Snapshot, to Videos\Panecast and Pictures\Panecast. A snapshot is also copied to the clipboard.
  • Settings (receiver name, quality, audio output, theme, language and similar) are saved in the local data folder.
  • Diagnostic logs (errors and crash details) are saved in the local data folder for 7 days. They can include the receiver name and the names of devices that connected. They are only shared if you choose to send them to us yourself.

Uninstalling the app removes its local data folder. Recordings and snapshots stay in your Videos and Pictures folders until you delete them.

Purchases, the trial and app ratings are handled by the Microsoft Store. The app asks the Store only whether it is licensed. Microsoft's handling of that data is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Política de privacidad (Español)

Panecast funciona solo en tu red local y no recopila, sube ni comparte información personal. No hay cuenta, análisis, publicidad ni seguimiento, y la app no se conecta a internet por su cuenta. Para que tus dispositivos encuentren esta PC, Panecast la anuncia en la red local con su nombre de receptor (por defecto, tu nombre de usuario de Windows seguido de "PC"; puedes cambiarlo). La imagen y el sonido que envía tu dispositivo se reproducen al llegar y no se guardan, salvo que grabes o hagas una captura (en Videos\Panecast e Imágenes\Panecast). Los nombres y tipos de los dispositivos que se conectaron, tus decisiones de permitir o bloquear, la configuración y los registros de diagnóstico (7 días) se guardan solo en la carpeta local de la app. Las compras, el período de evaluación y las valoraciones los gestiona Microsoft Store.

Datenschutzerklärung (Deutsch)

Panecast arbeitet nur in Ihrem lokalen Netzwerk und erhebt, überträgt oder teilt keine personenbezogenen Daten. Es gibt kein Konto, keine Analyse, keine Werbung und kein Tracking; die App baut selbst keine Internetverbindungen auf. Damit Ihre Geräte diesen PC finden, kündigt Panecast ihn im lokalen Netzwerk unter seinem Empfängernamen an (standardmäßig aus Ihrem Windows-Benutzernamen gebildet; jederzeit änderbar). Bild und Ton Ihres Geräts werden beim Empfang abgespielt und nicht gespeichert, außer Sie nehmen auf oder erstellen einen Schnappschuss (unter Videos\Panecast und Bilder\Panecast). Namen und Typen verbundener Geräte, Ihre Entscheidungen zum Zulassen oder Blockieren, Einstellungen und Diagnoseprotokolle (7 Tage) werden nur im lokalen App-Datenordner gespeichert. Kauf, Testzeitraum und Bewertungen wickelt der Microsoft Store ab.

隐私政策（简体中文）

Panecast 只在你的局域网内工作，不收集、不上传、也不分享任何个人信息。应用没有账户、统计、广告或跟踪，也不会自行连接互联网。为了让你的设备找到这台电脑，Panecast 会在局域网内以接收器名称广播这台电脑（默认由你的 Windows 用户名生成，可随时修改）。设备发送的画面和声音收到即播放，除非你录屏或截图（保存到“视频\Panecast”和“图片\Panecast”），否则不会保存。连接过的设备名称和类型、你的允许或阻止选择、设置以及诊断日志（保留 7 天）只保存在本机的应用数据文件夹中。购买、试用和评分由 Microsoft Store 处理。
